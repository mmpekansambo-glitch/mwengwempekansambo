## Front matter

- `title`, `date`, and `summary` are required.
- `coverImage` is optional; use a path such as `/images/my-cover.jpg`.
- `tags` is an optional list of short labels.
- `interests` is an optional list of interest ids, such as `[financial-stem, stories]`; use the ids shown in the portfolio's Interests page data.
- `draft: true` keeps the post out of production builds.

## Writing in Markdown

Use `##` and `###` for section headings, blank lines between paragraphs, and `-` for bullet lists. You can also add links, blockquotes, and images with standard Markdown syntax.
